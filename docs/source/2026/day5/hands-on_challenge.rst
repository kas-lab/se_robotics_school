*******************************************
Hands-On Challenge!
*******************************************
   *09:00 - 14:00* -- Carlos Hernandez Corbato, Elvin Alberts, MIRTE Support Team

**Session description:**
In this session, students are challenged to combine and apply the knowledge of the previous days.
Specificially, they are asked to combine the PDDL planning from Day 2 with the cognitive navigation system from Day 3 to make a MIRTE robot autonomously navigate in the school environment.

The session closes with demonstrations of the groups' solutions and rewarding the best performing solution with a prize.
