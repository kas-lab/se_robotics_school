Summer School for Systems and Software Engineering in Robotics 2026
===================================================================


   
   The 3rd edition of the Systems Software Engineering in Robotics Summer School is a fun week of lectures, keynotes, hands-on sessions and social activities around robot software engineering, hosted by the TU Delft Robotics Institute at Robohouse, in the campus of the Delft University of Technology, in the Netherlands. The school takes place from 29 June to 3 July 2026.

   The theme of the school incorporate "systems engineering" from this edition, with a focus on Cognitive Robot Systems Engineering based on the methods developed in the EU HORIZON Europe project CoreSense (https://coresense.eu/).

   One of the highlights of the school is the hands-on the participants enjoyed using the Mirte Master robot, thanks to the TU Delft Robotics MIRTE team (https://mirte.org/).
